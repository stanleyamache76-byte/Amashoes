About AMASHOES

Shoes for real days, chosen by hand in Kiserian.

AMASHOES started in Kiserian with one idea: shoes that keep up with real days — school runs, market trips, long commutes, and everything in between. We stock kids, women's and men's footwear chosen for comfort first, style second, and durability always.

Every pair on this site is picked by hand. If it wouldn't survive a Kiserian rainy season, it doesn't make the cut. From the first sketch of this shop to the last stitch on a shoebox, we've kept one question in mind: would we put this on our own kids, or wear it ourselves on a long day? If the answer is yes, it earns a place on these shelves.

What we stand for

Three things guide every pair we choose to stock.

Comfort first

Every shoe is walked in before it's listed — if it's not comfortable by the second wear, it doesn't stay in the catalog.

Built to last

Stitching, soles and materials chosen for Kiserian's roads and rainy seasons — not just for a nice photo.

Real, local service

Visit us in Kiserian Town for sizing help, exchanges, or just to talk shoes — we're neighbors first, a shop second.

Our services

Beyond just selling shoes.

Free in-store fitting

Not sure of a size? Bring the kids in — our team measures properly instead of guessing off a chart.

Easy exchanges

Wrong size or color arrived? Swap it at the Kiserian store or reach out through the Contact page.

Bulk & school orders

Kitting out a whole class or team? Message us for bulk pricing on kids' school shoes.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address. If you check out as a guest, we collect the name, email and phone number you provide for that order. Product reviews you submit are linked to your account. We do not collect payment card details directly — those are handled by our payment provider, not stored on this site.

How we store it

Your data is stored with Supabase, our backend provider, inside a Postgres database protected by row-level security — meaning our own database rules stop any customer from reading another customer's cart, orders, or account details, even if there were a bug elsewhere on the site.

Cookies & local storage

Before you log in, items you add to your cart are kept in your browser's local storage only — not sent anywhere — until you check out. We don't use third-party advertising trackers.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time by reaching out through the Contact page. Deleting your account removes your profile and order history from our active systems.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@amashoes.co.ke or through our Contact page.

Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026

Orders

Placing an order is an offer to buy at the listed price. We'll confirm your order and delivery details before it ships. Prices are listed in Kenyan Shillings (KES) and may change without notice; the price at the time you order is the one you pay.

Accounts

You're responsible for keeping your login details secure. Guest checkouts don't require a password, but the order details you provide (name, email, phone) are used solely to fulfil that order.

Reviews

Reviews should reflect a genuine experience with the product. We reserve the right to remove reviews that are abusive, fraudulent, or unrelated to the product.

Availability

Stock is shown as accurately as we can manage, but availability isn't guaranteed until an order is confirmed. If an item you ordered turns out to be unavailable, we'll contact you with options.

Liability

We aim for accuracy in product descriptions and images, but minor variations (e.g. shade of a color) can occur between photos and the physical product.

Returns Policy

Last updated: 2026

Return window

You can return an unworn pair, in its original box, within 7 days of delivery for an exchange or store credit. Bring your order confirmation (or order number) with you.

How to return

Visit us in-store in Kiserian Town, or reach out through the Contact page to arrange a return if you're outside the local area.

Condition

Shoes must be unworn, with tags and packaging intact, to qualify for a return. Items showing signs of wear can only be assessed case-by-case for manufacturing defects.

Refunds

Approved returns are processed as store credit by default; refunds to your original payment method can be requested and are processed within a few business days of the item being received.
